Product Placement Frequency Guide

Rule of 3: Each featured product appears a maximum of 3 times per week across all platforms. First mention is educational (ingredient/benefit), second is social proof (review/testimonial), third is promotional (offer/CTA). This prevents fatigue while building purchase consideration.

Annual Content Distribution

Annual baseline: Health Education (30%) | Product Spotlight (25%) | Community & Lifestyle (20%) | Promotions (15%) | Behind the Counter (10%)

Monthly ratios shift based on seasonal hooks: Promotional content peaks during Diwali (November, up to 25%) and Christmas (December, up to 25%).
