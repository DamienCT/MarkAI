Brand Positioning

Brand Archetype
The Caregiver — Driven by the desire to protect and care for others. MedActiv embodies the trusted health partner who anticipates needs, offers expert guidance, and makes wellness accessible to every Mauritian family.
Emotional Territory
Reassurance & Empowerment — "You are in good hands, and we will help you take control of your health." Every touchpoint should make customers feel both cared for and capable.

Your trusted neighbourhood health partner — combining pharmaceutical expertise with genuine care, making quality healthcare accessible to every family in Mauritius. We bridge the gap between clinical precision and community warmth.

Target Audiences

Cross-referenced from Research Report personas

Health-Conscious Millennials
Research persona: Priya, the Wellness Enthusiast
Young professionals (28-35) in urban Mauritius seeking natural wellness products and preventive health education. Bilingual French/English, digitally active, influenced by social proof. Respond best to educational carousels and Reels with ingredient breakdowns. Primary revenue opportunity in supplements and natural wellness.
Instagram (primary)FacebookWhatsApp
Fitness Enthusiasts
Research persona: Sophie, the Fitness Millennial
Active gym-goers (22-28) seeking protein, pre-workout, and recovery products. Price-sensitive but brand-loyal once converted. Influenced by peer reviews and influencer endorsements. Highest share rate of any segment. Key channel for organic reach amplification via UGC.
Instagram (primary)TikTokFacebook
Family Health Managers
Research persona: Rajesh, the Family Provider
Parents (40-50) managing household health. Value convenience, trust, and competitive pricing. Highest lifetime value segment. Respond to practical content: family health bundles, medication guides, chronic condition management. WhatsApp ordering is the key conversion unlock.
Facebook (primary)WhatsApp
Senior Wellness Seekers
Research persona: Marie-Claire, the Retiree
Retirees (60-75) managing chronic conditions. Prefer simple, Kreol-first communication. Lowest digital literacy but highest brand loyalty once engaged. Home delivery and WhatsApp refill service are critical retention tools. Content must use large text, simple language, and reassuring tone.
FacebookWhatsApp
Healthcare Professionals
Research persona: Dr. Anil, the Healthcare Professional
Doctors and practitioners who refer patients. Value clinical evidence and professional-grade products. Smallest audience but highest per-referral value. LinkedIn and email newsletter are the only effective channels. Content must be evidence-based and clinically rigorous.
