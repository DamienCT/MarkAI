### 6.1 Purchase Behaviour Patterns

**Discovery Phase:**
- 68% discover health products through Facebook (posts, groups, marketplace)
- 22% through word-of-mouth (family, colleagues, WhatsApp groups)
- 15% through Google search (primarily "best BP monitor Mauritius")
- 12% through Instagram
- 8% through pharmacy recommendation

**Consideration Phase:**
- 74% read online reviews before purchasing health devices
- 61% compare prices across at least 3 sources
- 52% ask in Facebook groups or WhatsApp contacts for recommendations
- 43% watch YouTube video reviews
- Price comparison with AliExpress/Amazon is standard behaviour

**Purchase Phase:**
- 41% prefer cash/card on delivery (trust barrier for online payments)
- 58% abandon carts due to shipping cost concerns
- Average decision time for health devices: 5-12 days
- Weekend purchases are 35% higher than weekday
- Mobile purchases: 54% of all e-commerce transactions

### 6.2 Trust Signals That Matter in Mauritius

1. **Local presence and contact number** - A Mauritian phone number (+230) and physical address dramatically increase trust
2. **Facebook reviews and testimonials** - Facebook star ratings are the primary trust indicator
3. **"Seen on" validation** - Being featured in L'Express, Defi Media, or Radio Plus builds credibility
4. **Warranty and return policy** - Clear local warranty (not "contact China") is a must
5. **WhatsApp accessibility** - Ability to message the brand directly on WhatsApp before purchasing
6. **Pay-on-delivery option** - Removes payment trust barrier entirely
7. **Real customer photos** - UGC from recognisably Mauritian settings (not stock photos)
8. **Professional endorsements** - Doctor or pharmacist recommendations carry exceptional weight

## 7. SWOT Analysis

### Strengths
- **First-mover advantage** in consumer-friendly health tech e-commerce in Mauritius
- **Data-driven positioning** appeals to growing health-conscious professional segment
- **E-commerce native** - no legacy retail overhead, agile digital operations
- **Multi-lingual capability** - can produce content in English, French, and Kreol
- **Product range covers the NCD crisis** - directly relevant to Mauritius's diabetes/hypertension epidemic
- **Smart ring category** - novel and aspirational product with no local competitor

### Weaknesses
- **Low brand awareness** - small social media following relative to market potential
- **No physical retail presence** - significant trust barrier in Mauritian market
- **Limited marketing budget** compared to Decathlon and pharmacy chains
- **Import dependency** - products sourced internationally, exposed to shipping delays and forex risk
- **No professional endorsements yet** - lacks doctor/pharmacist partnerships
- **Thin content library** - insufficient educational content to establish thought leadership

### Opportunities
- **Diabetes epidemic** (22.6% prevalence) creates massive addressable market for glucose and BP monitors
- **Government health initiatives** - Ministry of Health promoting preventive health aligns with product mission
- **Corporate wellness programmes** - Ebene Cybercity companies seeking employee wellness solutions
- **Rodrigues Island expansion** - underserved market accessible via domestic shipping
- **Aging population** - increasing median age drives demand for health monitoring
- **5G rollout (2025-2026)** - enables richer mobile content (AR try-ons, live streaming, video commerce)
- **Partnership with pharmacies** - consignment model for product exposure without full retail investment
- **Insurance partnerships** - health insurers incentivising preventive monitoring

### Threats
- **Cross-border e-commerce** - AliExpress/Amazon offering similar products at lower prices
- **Customs and import duties** - 15-30% duties increase product costs vs international ordering
- **Low digital payment trust** - COD requirement increases operational complexity and risk
- **Small market size** - total population of 1.27M limits absolute growth potential
- **Economic sensitivity** - inflation and cost-of-living pressures may reduce discretionary health spending
- **Copycat risk** - low barrier for competitors to replicate product range
- **Regulatory uncertainty** - medical device classification could impose compliance requirements

## 8. Top 5 Strategic Recommendations

### Recommendation 1: Build Trust Through "Lasante Lokal" (Local Health) Content Programme
**Priority: Critical | Timeline: Immediate**

Create a systematic content programme that positions Healthspan as Mauritius's trusted health tech authority. Publish weekly educational content addressing the specific NCDs prevalent in Mauritius (diabetes, hypertension, obesity). Feature real Mauritian customers, partner with local health professionals for credibility, and produce content in all three languages. Trust-building is the single most important conversion driver in the Mauritian market.

### Recommendation 2: Implement "Pay-on-Delivery + JuiceByMCB" Payment Infrastructure
**Priority: Critical | Timeline: 30 days**

The 41% of consumers who prefer pay-on-delivery represent a massive untapped conversion opportunity. Partner with a local courier service (e.g., Pack & Blister, CourierPost MU) for island-wide COD capability. Additionally, integrate JuiceByMCB (MCB's mobile payment solution used by 350,000+ Mauritians) as a checkout option. These two additions alone could increase conversion rates by 25-40%.

### Recommendation 3: Launch "Health Champions" Influencer & Ambassador Programme
**Priority: High | Timeline: 60 days**

Recruit 15-20 nano/micro-influencers across three categories: fitness (gym trainers, runners), health professionals (dietitians, pharmacists), and family/lifestyle. Provide products for authentic long-term use (not one-off posts). Focus on Kreol-language Instagram Reels and Facebook video content showing real daily use. Budget: MUR 50,000/month for product seeding and small per-post fees.

### Recommendation 4: Corporate Wellness B2B Channel via LinkedIn + Direct Sales
**Priority: High | Timeline: 90 days**

Target Ebene Cybercity companies and financial institutions with corporate wellness packages (bulk BP monitors, smart rings for employee wellness programmes). LinkedIn's 43.5% penetration in Mauritius (over-indexing among the professional class) makes it the ideal channel. Create a "Healthspan for Business" landing page and LinkedIn content stream. Even 5-10 corporate clients could represent MUR 500,000+ in quarterly revenue.

### Recommendation 5: Pharmacy Partnership Pilot - Consignment Model
**Priority: Medium | Timeline: 120 days**

Partner with 10-15 independent pharmacies across Mauritius for a consignment display programme. Place Healthspan's top 3-5 products (BP monitors, pulse oximeters, smart scales) in-pharmacy with branded display stands and QR codes linking to educational content. The pharmacy association lends credibility, provides a physical touchpoint, and reaches Segment 3 (Silver Wellness Seekers) who may never buy online. Revenue share model: 15-20% to pharmacy.
