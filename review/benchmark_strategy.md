## 1. Brand Voice & Tone

### 1.1 Voice Identity

Healthspan speaks as a **knowledgeable friend who genuinely cares about your family's health** - not a clinical authority and not a hype-driven marketer. The voice is warm, informed, empowering, and distinctly Mauritian.

**Core Voice Attributes:**

| Attribute | Description | Example |
|-----------|-------------|---------|
| **Empowering** | Give people control over their health data | "Now you can track your blood pressure at home - no more waiting at the clinic." |
| **Approachable** | Avoid medical jargon; speak like a well-informed friend | "Your heart rate tells a story. Let's learn to read it together." |
| **Locally Rooted** | Unapologetically Mauritian in references and language | "Kouma ou grandmer ti dir: prevenir vaut mieux ki guérir." |
| **Trustworthy** | Back claims with data; never overstate product capabilities | "Clinically validated to +/- 3mmHg accuracy. That's the same standard your doctor uses." |
| **Inclusive** | Health is for every body, every age, every budget | "Whether you're 25 or 75, Curepipe or Rodrigues - your health matters." |

### 1.2 Trilingual Content Framework

Healthspan operates in Mauritius's trilingual reality. Language choice is strategic, not random.

| Language | When to Use | Tone | Platforms |
|----------|------------|------|-----------|
| **Kreol Morisien** | Engagement posts, Reels/video narration, community replies, emotional content, call-to-actions | Warm, familiar, *"parey kouma to koz ar to kamarad"* | Instagram Reels, Facebook posts, WhatsApp, TikTok |
| **French** | Educational content, product descriptions, blog posts, longer captions, paid ads | Professional yet accessible, *"informatif mais pas ennuyeux"* | Facebook, Instagram captions, YouTube descriptions, blog |
| **English** | Technical specifications, SEO content, LinkedIn, B2B communications, website | Clean, authoritative, data-forward | LinkedIn, website, YouTube titles, Google Ads |

**Trilingual Caption Formula (Instagram/Facebook):**
```
[Hook in Kreol - 1 line to grab attention]
[Body in French - 2-3 lines of value/education]
[CTA in Kreol - direct, actionable]
[Hashtags in English + French + Kreol mix]
```

**Example:**
> Ou kone ki ou tension arteriel pe dir ou? 🩺
>
> L'hypertension touche 1 Mauricien sur 3. Pourtant, la plupart ne mesurent leur tension que lors d'une visite chez le médecin - soit 1 ou 2 fois par an.
>
> Avec un tensiomètre digital à domicile, vous pouvez suivre votre tension chaque jour et partager les données avec votre médecin.
>
> Check link dan bio pou dekouver nou BP Monitor range. Delivery partou dan Moris. 🇲🇺

## 2. Content Pillars

### Pillar Allocation

| # | Pillar | Allocation | Description | Primary Platform |
|---|--------|-----------|-------------|-----------------|
| 1 | **Edikasyon Lasante** (Health Education) | 30% | Evidence-based health tips, disease prevention, understanding health metrics, Mauritius-specific health data | Facebook, YouTube |

| 5 | **Deryer Rido** (Behind the Scenes) | 10% | Team stories, packing orders, product sourcing, brand values, warehouse life | Instagram Stories, Facebook |
| 6 | **Kominote & Kiltir** (Community & Culture) | 5% | Health awareness days, local events, Mauritian cultural health practices, CSR | Facebook, Instagram |

### Pillar Detail

#### Pillar 1: Edikasyon Lasante (30%)
**Goal:** Establish Healthspan as Mauritius's most trusted source of consumer health information.

**Content Types:**
- "Health Metric Monday" - weekly explanation of a health metric (resting heart rate, SpO2, HRV, blood pressure ranges)
- Infographics: Mauritius NCD statistics presented visually
- "Ask the Expert" - video Q&A with local health professionals
- Myth-busting posts (e.g., "Non, manz bred avek dimiel pa geri diabetes")
- Seasonal health tips (cyclone season preparedness, summer hydration, winter immunity)

**Mauritius Localisation:**
- Reference Ministry of Health data and local hospital statistics
- Address Mauritius-specific diet concerns (high sugar/oil in traditional cooking, dholl puri nutritional breakdown)
- Partner with SSRN Hospital, Wellkin Hospital, or City Clinic doctors for credibility

#### Pillar 2: Prodwi & Teknolozi (25%)
**Goal:** Educate on product features and drive consideration/conversion.

**Content Types:**
- Product demo videos (30-60 seconds, Kreol narration)
- "How to set up your [product]" tutorials
- Comparison posts (analog vs digital BP monitor, basic vs smart scale)
- Feature spotlights (app connectivity, data sharing with doctor)
- "What's in the box" unboxing content
- Price justification posts ("Why our smart ring costs Rs 8,000 and it's worth every roupi")

#### Pillar 3: Listwer Nou Kliyan (15%)
**Goal:** Build social proof and community through real Mauritian stories.

**Content Types:**
- Video testimonials (30-60 seconds, customer's own words in Kreol/French)
- "A day in my health journey" - following a customer's daily use
- Photo features with customer permission
- Screenshot shares of positive WhatsApp feedback (with consent)
- "1 month with my smart ring" long-term review series

#### Pillar 4: Lavi Aktif Moris (15%)
**Goal:** Associate Healthspan with the active, healthy Mauritian lifestyle.

**Content Types:**
- "Workout Wednesday" - exercises that pair with health tracking
- Healthy Mauritian recipe adaptations (lighter vindaye, sugar-free gateau patate)
- Local fitness spot guides (hike Le Morne with heart rate tracking, run the Caudan waterfront)
- Yoga/meditation content with local instructors
- "Weekend warrior" community fitness challenges

#### Pillar 5: Deryer Rido (10%)
**Goal:** Humanise the brand; show the real people behind Healthspan.

**Content Types:**
- Packing and shipping process videos
- Team introductions and "day in the life"
- Product quality checking and testing
- Warehouse/office behind-the-scenes
- "Why we started Healthspan" founder story

#### Pillar 6: Kominote & Kiltir (5%)
**Goal:** Show that Healthspan is part of the Mauritian community, not just selling to it.

**Content Types:**
- Health awareness day activations (World Health Day, World Diabetes Day, World Heart Day)
- Community health screening partnerships
- Mauritian cultural health practices (tisane, herbal remedies - respectful, not dismissive)
- CSR initiatives (donate a BP monitor to an elderly home)
- Festival-linked content (Diwali wellness, Eid health tips, Christmas family health)

## 6. Engagement Strategy

### 6.1 Response Protocols

| Interaction Type | Response Time Target | Response Protocol | Language |
|-----------------|---------------------|-------------------|----------|
| **Purchase inquiry** (DM/Comment) | < 30 minutes (business hours) | Acknowledge, answer, link to product, offer WhatsApp | Match customer's language |
| **Positive comment/review** | < 2 hours | Thank personally, ask permission to share, engage further | Kreol preferred |
| **Negative comment/complaint** | < 1 hour | Acknowledge publicly, move to DM/WhatsApp, resolve | Empathetic, customer's language |
| **Health question** | < 4 hours | Provide general info, disclaimer ("consult your doctor"), link to resource | French or Kreol |
| **Tagged/UGC content** | < 4 hours | Like, comment, ask to reshare, add to UGC library | Kreol |
| **Influencer/media inquiry** | < 24 hours | Personalised response, arrange call/meeting | English or French |

### 6.2 Community Building Tactics

**Daily Engagement Routine (30 minutes/day):**
1. Respond to all comments and DMs from past 12 hours
2. Like and comment on 10 posts from followers

4. Like and comment on 5 posts from local influencers/health professionals
5. Check brand mentions and tagged posts; engage or reshare

**Weekly Engagement Initiatives:**
- Monday: Health poll on Instagram Stories ("Ou check ou tension arteriel regularly?")
- Wednesday: "Ask Us Anything" Q&A in Instagram Stories
- Friday: Share a UGC or customer testimonial with credit

## Appendix: Content Production Workflow

### Workflow Steps

1. **Ideation** (Monday) - Weekly content brainstorm based on pillar allocation, trending topics, upcoming events
2. **Briefing** (Monday-Tuesday) - Create content briefs with copy, visual direction, hashtags, and platform specs
3. **Production** (Tuesday-Thursday) - Photography, videography, graphic design, copywriting
4. **Review** (Thursday) - Quality check: brand voice, factual accuracy, language correctness, visual standards
5. **Scheduling** (Friday) - Load approved content into scheduling tool (Later, Hootsuite, or Meta Business Suite)
6. **Publishing** (Automated) - Posts go live at scheduled times
7. **Engagement** (Ongoing) - Monitor and respond within SLA targets
8. **Analysis** (Weekly) - Performance review, identify top/bottom performers, adjust strategy
