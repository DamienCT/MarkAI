## English

See how food, sleep & stress affect your glucose — in real time.

The SiBionics GS1 is a continuous glucose monitor that sits discreetly on your arm and tracks your levels for 14 days straight. No finger pricks, no interruptions — just a steady stream of data to help you make better decisions about what you eat and how you live.

Understanding your glucose response is one of the most powerful things you can do for long-term health. This makes it easy.
